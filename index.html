How it works

From dream to journal in three steps.

A travel companion that grows with every trip you take.

01

Plan

Generate full itineraries with AI in seconds, or build them by hand with six block types: places, food, activities, flights, housing & notes.

02

Track

Pin every city you've visited and watch your interactive 3D globe fill in over time.

03

Share

Publish trips for the community, save inspiration from others, and share with friends.

Generate

A full itinerary, written for you in seconds.

Tell Roamly where you're going, when, and what you're into, and get a complete day-by-day plan with real places, food picks, and time blocks. Flight-aware scheduling means your first and last days actually match your travel times, not generic templates.

  • Personalized to your destination, dates & interests
  • Flight-aware day planning, not cookie-cutter templates
  • Edit anything, regenerate sections, or start from scratch
Plan trips

A day-by-day builder for the way you actually travel.

Time-blocked schedules with six activity types: places, food, activities, flights, housing, and notes. Add photos, links, and details to every stop. Roamly keeps everything in sync across your devices.

  • Six block types for every kind of plan
  • Photos, notes, links & precise times
  • Auto-saves & syncs in real time
Track

An interactive 3D globe for every adventure.

Pin the cities you've visited and explore them on a beautiful, satellite-rendered globe. Spin, zoom, and tap any pin to revisit your trip. Your travel history, made tangible.

  • Real-world satellite globe powered by Apple Maps
  • Country & city tracking with one tap
  • See visited countries highlighted on the globe
Discover

Real itineraries from real travelers.

Browse trips published by the Roamly community. Search by destination, sort by top-rated or newest, and bookmark anything that inspires your next adventure.

  • Community-published trips, ranked by votes
  • Filter by destination, duration & activities
  • Save trips to revisit when you're ready to plan
Share

Publish your trips. Inspire someone else.

Share your itineraries publicly with one tap, or send a private link to a friend. See how many travelers loved your trip and watch your library of journals grow.

  • One-tap publish to the Roamly community
  • Private share links for friends & family
  • Track hearts & activity on every post

About

Hi, I'm Alex.

I built Roamly because I love traveling and wanted a single place to plan trips, remember where I've been, and find inspiration from other travelers. What started as a simple 3D globe journal has grown into the full travel planning & community app you're looking at today.

I'm a software engineer who treats app-building as my favorite thing to do. Outside of code you'll find me on a basketball court, a golf course, the slopes, or in the gym, usually with music on.
